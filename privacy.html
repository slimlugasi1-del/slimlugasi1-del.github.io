Privacy Policy

ForgeHub is currently a static website. We do not currently provide user accounts, store model workspaces, or collect form submissions.

Information and services

The site loads public model metadata from the Hugging Face Hub API when the live catalog is enabled. Your browser may also request fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may process requests according to their own policies.

Advertising

ForgeHub may display Google AdSense advertising after the site is approved and activated. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. You can manage advertising preferences through Google’s ad settings and your browser controls.

Links to other sites

Model cards, agents, documentation, and other resources may link to third-party websites. ForgeHub is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

Your choices

You can disable cookies through your browser settings. If you have a privacy question, please use the public project contact channel on the Contact page.
