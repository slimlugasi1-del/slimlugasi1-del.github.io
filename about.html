ABOUT FORGEHUB

Open model intelligence, made clearer.

ForgeHub is an independent discovery and learning interface for open models, agent workflows, and customization tools.

Our goal is to help builders compare model capabilities, understand tradeoffs, and find practical starting points for local and hosted AI projects.

What you’ll find here

  • Curated model metadata and community labels
  • Summaries of models, agents, and customization workflows
  • Learning resources for building with open AI systems

ForgeHub is not affiliated with Hugging Face, the model authors listed in the catalog, or any third-party provider unless explicitly stated.
